## Application Strategy

### Priority Order (Based on Speed & Alignment)
1. **SSBCI Working Capital** — Fastest path to capital ($50K, no equity, credit support)
2. **Florida High Tech Corridor R&D** — $150K for tech + research partnership  
3. **Pitch Factory** — $1K + validation + mentorship + community access
4. **Repeat** — Quarterly cycles for maximum funding

### Detailed Application Paths

#### **SSBCI Working Capital**
- **Administered By:** FloridaCommerce
- **Total Allocation:** $488 million (Florida), $142M first tranche deployed
- **Program Type:** Credit support (loans, VC, loan guarantees, collateral support), NOT direct grants
- **Eligibility:** Florida-based business, typically 2-100 employees
- **Funding Types Available:**
  - Collateral Support Program: Fills collateral shortfalls
  - Loan Participation Program: Pairs SSBCI funds with private lenders
  - Loan Guarantee Program: Partial guarantees for business lines of credit
  - Capital Access Program: Pooled loan insurance via participating lenders
  - Equity Capital Program: Venture capital partnerships for high-growth startups
- **How to Apply:** Through network of participating lenders/partner organizations
- **Best For:** Immediate working capital without equity dilution
- **Action Step:** Contact FloridaCommerce for current lender list

#### **Florida High Tech Corridor R&D Grant**
- **Administered By:** Florida High Tech Corridor Office
- **Maximum Award:** $150,000
- **Match Requirement:** Cost-sharing with university researchers (UCF, USF, FSU, FAMU)
- **Eligible Activities:** R&D, product development, pilot projects, technology transfer
- **Application Timeline:** Quarterly cycles (typically Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct)
- **Best For:** Technology development with academic validation
- **Action Step:** Identify university researcher partner, develop joint proposal

#### **Pitch Factory (St. Johns County Chamber)**
- **Award:** Up to $1,000 cash prize
- **Program Length:** 6-week entrepreneurial training program
- **Application Timeline:** Typically Q1 each year
- **Eligibility:** Early-stage startups in St. Johns County
- **Benefits:** $1K prize + 6-week training + mentorship + demo day + networking
- **Best For:** Validation, skill building, initial credibility
- **Action Step:** Monitor chamber website for application opening

#### **Tampa Bay Wave Accelerator**
- **Program:** #3 ranked startup accelerator in US (TIME/Statista 2026)
- **Award:** Equity investment + mentorship + resources
- **Eligibility:** Tech startups with MVP or early traction
- **Benefits:** Seed funding, office access, mentorship network, demo day
- **Application Timeline:** Rolling with specific cohort dates
- **Best For:** Scaling, mentorship, investor connections
- **Action Step:** Prepare strong application with traction metrics

## Application Best Practices

### Documentation to Prepare
1. **Business Plan:** Executive summary, market analysis, financial projections
2. **Team Bios:** Founders and key advisors with relevant experience
3. **Financial Statements:** Current P&L, balance sheet, cash flow (if available)
4. **Use of Funds:** Detailed breakdown of how grant money will be spent
5. **Impact Metrics:** How you'll measure success and community benefit
6. **Sustainability Plan:** How project continues after grant period
7. **Letters of Support:** From partners, customers, community leaders

### Timeline Management
- **Quarterly Planning:** Align applications with fiscal quarters
- **Buffer Time:** Allow 2-4 weeks for application preparation
- **Follow-up:** Track status and follow up appropriately
- **Reporting:** Prepare for post-award reporting requirements

### Success Rates & Expectations
- **SSBCI:** High approval rate for eligible businesses (~70-80%)
- **Florida High Tech Corridor:** Moderate (~40-50%) with strong university partner
- **Pitch Factory:** Competitive but accessible (~20-30% acceptance)
- **Tampa Bay Wave:** Selective (~10-15%) but high value
- **Federal Grants (SBIR/STTR):** Low (~10-15%) but high reward

## Risk Mitigation in Grant Strategy

### Application Fatigue
- **Mitigation:** Focus on 2-3 high-probability applications/quarter
- **Mitigation:** Reuse and adapt materials across applications
- **Mitigation:** Set realistic goals (2-4 applications/quarter max)

### Reporting Burden
- **Mitigation:** Choose grants with lighter reporting requirements
- **Mitigation:** Implement tracking systems early
- **Mitigation:** Assign grant management to specific team member

### Mission Drift
- **Mitigation:** Only apply to grants aligned with core mission
- **Mitigation:** Use grant funds strictly for proposed purposes
- **Mitigation:** Maintain strategic focus despite funding opportunities
