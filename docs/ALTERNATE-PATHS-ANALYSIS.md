## Recommended Paths

**Primary Recommendation: Path A + D (Commercial RE + Community-first)**
- **Why:** Best balance of speed, capital, and purpose alignment
- **Critical path:** Quick wins → validate → scale
- **Timeline:** 6-9 months to market, $25K-$50K initial capital

## Detailed Path Analysis

### Path A: Commercial Real Estate Specialization
**What:** Focus on commercial properties ($500K-$5M transactions)
**Why:** 5-10x higher commissions, less competition, AI advantage in complex deals
**How:** Target restaurants, retail, offices in the corridor
**Timeline:** 6-9 months to first deal
**Capital Required:** $25K
**Profitability:** One deal = $50K-$100K commission

### Path D: Community-First (Beach Club)
**What:** Build beach club membership community
**Why:** Recurring revenue, network effects, leads for brokerage
**How:** Monthly fee for young entrepreneurs (25-40 age range)
**Timeline:** 6-9 months to 50+ members
**Capital Required:** $10K-$25K
**Profitability:** $97/month × 25 members = $2,425/month recurring

### Hybrid Approach (A+D)
1. **Start with Beach Club** (Phase 1) to validate concept and build community
2. **Add Commercial Brokerage** (Phase 2) once community is established
3. **Cross-pollinate** beach club members become brokerage clients
4. **Scale** beach club to multiple locations once brokerage is proven

## Phase-Specific Path Recommendations

### Phase 1 (Months 1-3): Foundation & Quick Wins
- **Primary:** Path D (Beach Club)
- **Secondary:** Path G (Partnerships with REAL Brokerage, Consider It Closed)
- **Goal:** Validate model with minimal capital

### Phase 2 (Months 4-6): Validation & Scaling
- **Primary:** Path A (Commercial RE)
- **Secondary:** Path D (Scale beach club)
- **Goal:** Prove revenue model with first commercial deals

### Phase 3 (Months 7-12): Growth & Funding
- **Primary:** Paths A+D combined
- **Secondary:** Paths B or E (if tech differentiation is proven)
- **Goal:** Scale geographically, secure funding, build team
