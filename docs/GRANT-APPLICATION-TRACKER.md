### Application Workflow

1. **Identify** — Match opportunity to project phase (Phase 1: Foundation, Phase 2: Growth)
2. **Prepare** — Gather business plan, financials, team bios, technical specifications
3. **Submit** — Follow deadline protocols and submission channels
4. **Monitor** — Track status, respond to inquiries, prepare for reviews
5. **Report** — Document outcomes and lessons learned

## Phase-Specific Funding Strategy

### Phase 1 (Months 1-3): Foundation & Quick Wins
**Target Grants:** SSBCI, Florida High Tech Corridor R&D, Pitch Factory

**Funding Objectives:**
- Infrastructure setup (platform, data, tools)
- Quick win execution (4 proof points)
- Team foundation (core hires)
- Partnership onboarding (REAL, Consider It Closed, Antonio Pica)

**Funding Request Strategy:**
- **SSBCI:** Emphasize early-stage ecosystem viability and job creation potential
- **High Tech R&D:** Focus on innovation in AI model integration and market analysis
- **Pitch Factory:** Highlight community impact and local economic development

### Phase 2 (Months 4-6): Growth & Expansion
**Target Grants:** SBIR/STTR Phase 2, HIPI Grants

**Funding Objectives:**
- AI model development and optimization
- Talent acquisition and training
- Feature expansion (advanced analytics, predictive modeling)
- Partnership deepening (additional strategic partners)

**Funding Request Strategy:**
- **SBIR/STTR:** Demonstrate commercial readiness through Phase 1 quick wins
- **HIPI:** Align with IT and R&D focus areas (AI automation, data infrastructure)

### Phase 3 (Months 7-12): Maturity & Sustainability
**Target Grants:** SelectFlorida Trade Grants, continued SSBCI

**Funding Objectives:**
- Geographic expansion
- Product line extension (property management, data services)
- Capital raise preparation
- Legacy structure development

**Funding Request Strategy:**
- Position as proven, scalable business ready for capital efficiency
- Emphasize revenue traction and partnership ecosystem maturity
